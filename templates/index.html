AI Confidence Score Explanation

Each detected dimension is accompanied by a confidence score that represents how reliably the system identified that feature. This score helps users judge the trustworthiness of extracted measurements.

Edge Strength

Strong, continuous edges increase confidence, while broken or noisy edges reduce it.

Geometric Consistency

Dimensions matching expected geometric patterns (parallel lines, circular symmetry) score higher.

Detection Redundancy

Features detected multiple times across scales or orientations receive higher confidence.

Noise & Occlusion

Overlapping annotations, text, or noise reduce confidence values.
